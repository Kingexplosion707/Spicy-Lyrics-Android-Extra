Spicy Lyrics Plus for Steam (Millennium plugin)

Needs Millennium 3.4 or newer (https://steambrew.app) and Windows 10/11.

Install: run Install.cmd (or copy spicy-lyrics-plus.star into Steam\millennium\plugins),
restart Steam, then turn it on in Millennium > Plugins.

Use: play a song in Spotify (or any player that shows in Windows' media controls).
A lyrics panel appears in the Steam window. Drag it by the top, resize from the corner,
the expand button fills the window, the ... button has translation, text size and sync.
Ctrl+Shift+L hides/shows it; when hidden, a small "now playing" pill opens it again.
